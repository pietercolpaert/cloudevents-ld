    » producer-specific context

    context.jsonld maps the CloudEvents attributes and the extensions CloudEvents documents. Producers define other extension attributes; to keep them, map them here to predicates in the producer's namespace. This context is applied after context.jsonld and cannot redefine its terms.

    2 cloudevents-ld

    Applying context.jsonld turns each CloudEvent into one RDF Message, using the CloudEvents-LD vocabulary. Together they form an RDF Message Log. The data payload stays an opaque rdf:JSON literal: parse it separately, based on its datacontenttype and dataschema.

    3 align with another vocabulary

    Because the events are now Linked Data, they are not only interoperable with CloudEvents, but with any vocabulary that alignments.ttl links to. eyemind reasons over the vocabulary and its alignments, guided by shapes.ttl as the shape of each incoming message and by the target's shapes as the shape it must produce.
